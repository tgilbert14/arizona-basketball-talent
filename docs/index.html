The Body Map

Every player in the conference plotted by height × weight, your team highlighted by position. Tap any dot for the recruit's card.

Conference Beef

Leaderboards for weight, height, and pounds-per-inch — commit classes or current rosters, with a Trenches filter.

Player Cards

Tap a name in any pinned card and a holographic player card spins in — size, rating, hometown, miles from campus, and their 247 link.

🛡️

The War Room

Every defensive body mapped onto Arizona's 3-3-5 odd-stack roles — two-deep targets, incoming adds at projected weights, auto-written brief.

🏆

Talent vs Results

Recruiting composites joined to real season records and SP+ — who outplays their talent, and who wastes it.

📈

Coach Eras

Every head coach's recruiting fingerprint: ratings, blue-chips, size, footprint — era by era, class by class.

Built to be argued with

Pin cards to a chart, drag them where you want, and save the whole annotated view as a PNG — receipts included.

Data & transparency. Recruiting classes and rosters from 247Sports (2016–2026, portal transfers included from 2021); season records and SP+ from CollegeFootballData. Heights and weights are as reported by programs — treat them as directional, not lab-measured. Every chart in the app states its player pool and source, and every metric has a methods note. Found a data error? Tell us — data validity outranks everything else here.
