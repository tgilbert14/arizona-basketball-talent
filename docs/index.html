Put rivals on the same scale.

Move past roster averages. The head-to-head view reveals where one program is longer, heavier or more highly rated at the exact position groups that decide a matchup.

See what programs do with talent.

Recruiting is an input, not an outcome. The scoreboard brings composite talent, wins and SP+ together so development and underperformance are visible across seasons.

  • Season-level roster composites
  • Wins and advanced ratings
  • Coach-era context

Transparent by design

A method you can inspect, not a black box.

Every chart names its player pool, unit of analysis and source. The app separates reported measurements from modeled comparisons and keeps the caveats beside the result.

01 / COLLECT

Recruiting and rosters

247Sports classes and team rosters from 2016 through the current 2027 cycle, with portal additions included from 2021.

02 / NORMALIZE

Comparable positions

Team labels, positions, measurements and ratings are standardized so like-for-like comparisons remain possible.

03 / CONNECT

Results in context

Season records and SP+ from CollegeFootballData connect recruiting inputs to on-field football performance.

04 / DISCLOSE

Limits stay visible

Program-reported heights and weights are directional, not lab measurements. Samples and definitions travel with each view.
